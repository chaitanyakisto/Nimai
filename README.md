# Nimai Trading Ltd - Corporate Website

This repository contains the source code for the official website of **Nimai Trading Ltd**, a premier supplier of industrial packaging and labeling solutions based in Mauritius.

![Nimai Trading Ltd](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-In%20Development-blue)
![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/github/license/your-username/nimai-trading-website)

## 🚀 About The Project

Nimai Trading Ltd specializes in providing high-quality packaging and labeling products to businesses across Mauritius and the Indian Ocean region. This website serves as the company's digital storefront, showcasing its product range and generating qualified leads.

**Core Products Featured:**
- Thermal Transfer Ribbons
- Shipping & Direct Thermal Labels
- Polypropylene Strapping
- Paper Dunnage Air Bags

## 📋 Project Overview

The website is designed to be:
- **Professional & Trustworthy:** Reflecting the company's reputation for quality and reliability.
- **User-Centric:** Providing an intuitive experience for procurement managers, warehouse operators, and business owners.
- **Mobile-Responsive:** Ensuring optimal viewing on all devices.
- **SEO-Optimized:** Built to rank well in search results for relevant industrial terms in Mauritius.
